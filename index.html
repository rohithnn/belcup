Fair Play

Rules & Regulations

🛡️

All participants must adhere to the following rules. Violations may result in disqualification.

  1. 01
    Age Requirement

    Participants must be 15 years or older to register and compete.

  2. 02
    Non-Refundable Entry

    Entry fees are strictly non-refundable under any circumstances.

  3. 03
    Punctuality

    Players must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match.

  4. 04
    Referee Authority

    The referee's decision is final and binding. No disputes will be entertained.

  5. 05
    Conduct

    Misconduct or unsportsmanlike behavior leads to immediate disqualification.

  6. 06
    Liability

    The organizers are not responsible for any personal belongings or injuries sustained during the event.
